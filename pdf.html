Privacy Policy — RIG PRO PDF Viewer

Last updated: 13 August 2026

RIG PRO PDF Viewer (“the app”) is an offline PDF reader for field teams. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising. The app does not hold the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data anywhere even in principle.

What the app stores, and where

All data is written to the app’s private storage area on your device:

DataPurpose
The list of recently opened documents and their pathsReopening where you left off
Per-document view state (page, zoom, scroll position)Restoring your place
App settings and preferencesYour display and behaviour choices

The app reads the PDF files you explicitly open. It does not copy them, index them, scan your storage, or keep a copy of their contents.

No part of this data is uploaded anywhere. Automatic cloud backup is disabled (allowBackup="false"), so this data is not copied to Google Drive either. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. Documents reach the app only when you choose them yourself — through the system file picker, or by sending a PDF to the app from another app’s share sheet. Android grants access to that one file, for that one session.

Network access

The app makes no network requests and declares no network permission. It works identically in airplane mode.

Data sharing and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because none is collected.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Rendering

PDF pages are rendered on your device using Android’s built-in PdfRenderer and a PDF rendering library bundled inside the app. Document contents are never sent to Google or to any other server for processing.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above changes with it, and the updated policy is published at the same URL before the corresponding app version is released.
